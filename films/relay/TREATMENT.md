# Relay — "Pass it on" · Marker Paper · 8 s silent loop

Brief: portfolio intro film for Relay (team chat that runs standups itself). Silent (site video is muted): no voice, no music. 1920×1080, 24 fps, 8.0 s, last frame flows into the first. Plate mode (`?plate=img/x.jpg`, 6 s) frames any real screen the same way, for every image slot on the site.

Style: marker-paper, adapted to Relay's brand. Cream paper `#F8F5EC`, black marker `#1B1B1B`; the style's "given / wanted" pair becomes Relay's amber `#F5A400` (what the team asks) and pink `#F7B6D2` (what the bot hands back). Kalam for hand lettering, Patrick Hand for the word mark.

## Three candidate structures
1. **Baton** — three teammates in a ring pass a speech bubble hand to hand like a relay baton; each pass draws an arc; the bot stamps it done and a taped screenshot slides in. *Chosen*: the name is the metaphor, and a ring loops naturally.
2. **Monday** — a calendar's Monday box fills with meeting scribbles, then the bot crosses them all out. Strong, but ends on a state that does not loop.
3. **Peel stack** — taped screenshots peeled one by one. Shows the product, no story.

## Beat sheet (chosen)
| t | beat |
|---|---|
| 0.0–1.2 | Paper with wobble grid. Three teammates in a ring, bot in the centre. Amber bubble "standup?" above A. |
| 1.2–3.0 | Bubble passed A→B→C along arcs that draw themselves in marker (boil on every line). |
| 3.0–4.4 | C hands it to the bot; pink stamp "done ✓". A taped screenshot slides up, real UI. |
| 4.4–6.6 | Screenshot peels (corner curl) to a second screen, then to the title "Relay — team management, minus the meetings". |
| 6.6–8.0 | Title and arcs wipe away; bubble returns to A: identical to t=0. |

## Shot list
One locked top-down shot of the paper, slow 2 % breathing push. Transitions are peel and push-through inside the paper. No cuts.

Differs from the demo on: palette, subject, opening (figures present, no blank page), transition set, ending (loop), real UI plates.
