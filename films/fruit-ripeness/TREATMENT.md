# Ripe or Not — "Three yes/no questions" · Pop Art · 8 s silent loop

Brief: intro film for an ordinal-regression ripeness grader (unripe → partially ripe → ripe → rotten). Silent. 1920×1080, 24 fps, 8 s loop. Plate mode puts each real screen in a halftone comic panel.

Style: pop-art in the project's brand: hot pink `#EC0B9C`, yellow `#F9C34B`, ink `#141414`, cream `#FBF6EE`; Ben-Day dots in page space; Anton for stamps, Fraunces for the title.

## Three candidate structures
1. **The ruler** — a fruit marker rides a ripeness ruler 0→3; at each tick a stamped panel asks "> 0?" "> 1?" "> 2?" and stamps YES / YES / NO; verdict "RIPE!". *Chosen*: the ordinal idea in one image.
2. **Softmax vs ordinal** — four boxes shatter into a line. Harder to read in 8 s.
3. **Grad-CAM** — heat dots over the fruit. Used inside plate mode.

## Beat sheet
