# AI Research Assistant — "Five agents, one sourced report" · Dark Keynote · 8 s silent loop

Brief: intro film for a LangGraph five-agent research system. Silent, no voice. 1920×1080, 24 fps, 8 s loop. Plate mode shows each real screen as a keynote UI card.

Style: dark-keynote. Near-black `#0B0C10`, faint grid, text `#E8ECF2`, one accent: the product's violet `#8B7CFF` (teal `#2DD4BF` only for the single "pass" tick). Inter Tight display, JetBrains Mono labels.

## Three candidate structures
1. **The graph runs** — Planner → Searcher → Reader → Critic → Writer light in turn; the Critic sends the pulse back to the Searcher once (the self-correcting loop); the Writer expands into the real report. *Chosen*: it is the architecture, and the pulse can circle forever.
2. **Count-up** — "20 tabs → 1 report", number from glyph noise. Used as a beat inside (1).
3. **Terminal** — a streamed SSE log. Too small to read in a collage.

## Beat sheet
| t | beat |
|---|---|
| 0–1.5 | Grid; five mono-labelled nodes on a line. Pulse leaves Planner. |
| 1.5–3.5 | Searcher, Reader, Critic; Critic flags "gap", pulse loops back to Searcher (retry 1/2), then forward. |
| 3.5–6.0 | Writer expands into a card with the real report; "5 agents · 1 sourced report" resolves from glyph noise. |
| 6.0–8.0 | Card collapses into Writer; pulse returns to Planner; t=8 = t=0. |

Locked camera, 1.5 % push. Transitions are UI expand / collapse only.

Differs from the demo on: subject, structure, opening, ending (loop), accent, real screens.
