# Dynamic Island for Windows — "A living pill" · Glass Product · 8 s silent loop

Brief: intro film for a Windows app that puts a spring-animated island at the top of the screen. Silent. 1920×1080, 24 fps, 8 s loop. Plate mode shows each real island state on the glass pill.

Style: glass-product (three.js). The object is the user's own product: a black glass pill. Graphite stage, two strip lights, comet pulses along the rim in the brand's red `#D7263D` and blue `#2B59C3`. Inter Tight 200 for the single line of type.

## Three candidate structures
1. **States** — the pill springs from sliver → Now Playing → Notification → sliver; its face shows the real UI strips; a comet laps the rim at each change. *Chosen*: it is what the product does.
2. **Desk** — the pill on a floating monitor bezel. More context, less object.
3. **Exploded** — layers separating. Off-brief for a UI product.
