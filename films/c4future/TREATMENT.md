# C4Future — "The hidden number" · Engraving · 8 s silent loop

Brief: intro film for C4Future (product carbon-footprint model with honest error bars). Silent, no voice. 1920×1080, 24 fps, 8 s seamless loop. Plate mode frames each real screen as an engraved plate.

Style: copperplate engraving in C4Future's palette. Paper `#EBEEE7`, ink `#2B3A30`, one hand-colour wash in terracotta `#B4502A`. Fraunces (the product's own face) for the cartouche, Pinyon Script for the flourish line. Tone by line only.

## Three candidate structures
1. **Shirt's voyage** — a hatched globe turns; a freighter engraves its route from Shanghai; an engraved T-shirt takes a terracotta wash as the cartouche reveals "8.46 kg CO₂e" with a 90 % interval. *Chosen*: the product's real worked example; the route can loop.
2. **Ledger** — a balance weighing a shirt against coal. Metaphor over substance.
3. **Specimen plates** — screens as natural-history plates. Kept as plate mode.

## Beat sheet
| t | beat |
|---|---|
| 0–2 | Hatched globe turns (cross-hatch on the night side). Dotted sea route engraves; a small freighter rides it. |
| 2–4 | Burin cuts an engraved T-shirt; terracotta wash floods it. |
| 4–6 | Cartouche: "The hidden number", then "8.46 kg CO₂e" and the interval bracket as an engraved scale. |
| 6–8 | Wash drains, shirt lines retract, ship completes the circuit; the globe has turned one hatch period, so t=8 = t=0. |

## Shot list
One plate, locked, slow 3 % drift, ornamental border. No cuts.

Differs from the demo on: subject, palette, structure (data cartouche), opening (already in motion), ending (loop), real screens in plate mode.
